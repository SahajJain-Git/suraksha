The Suraksha Methodology

How Suraksha Builds Real Readiness

Disaster response is not about memorizing complex manuals—it is about forming calm, automated habits before emergencies occur.

1. Structured Lessons

Bite-sized, jargon-free lessons that explain the science of hazards and outline clear DOs and DON'Ts verified against emergency guidelines.

2. Virtual Simulations

Scenario-based mock drills present unexpected challenges, forcing learners to evaluate risks, choose exits, and review the consequences safely.

3. Verified Knowledge Checks

Objective quizzes scored on the server evaluate understanding, ensuring students achieve genuine readiness before receiving completion certificates.

Quick Reference Guide

The Three Golden Rules of Emergency Survival

Commit these fundamentals to memory. In an emergency, simple instinctive actions save lives.

1. In an Earthquake

DROP, COVER, HOLD ON

Drop to your hands and knees. Cover your head and neck under a sturdy table. Hold on until shaking stops. Never run outside while walls and glass are actively vibrating.

2. In Structural Smoke

CRAWL LOW UNDER SMOKE

Smoke and toxic hot gases rise to the ceiling. The cleanest, coolest air is 30 to 60 cm above the floor. Drop to your hands and crawl toward the nearest marked fire exit.

3. In Flash Flooding

TURN AROUND, DON'T DROWN

Just 15 cm of moving floodwater can knock an adult off their feet; 30 cm can float most passenger cars. Never attempt to walk, wade, or drive through flooded roads.
